| NEW-1 | P3 | VERIFIED-READ | **The confirm path now refuses images over 50 MP** (`service/image_pixels.go:26`, the dating cap). This is a deliberate trade against decompression bombs, but it refuses legitimate photos; there is no client message for it, and the asset is just marked failed. **Checked and found sound:**

- **Takedown queue:**
  - No double effect: the claim takes a 2 min lease with `SKIP LOCKED`, and post-service matches the decision id before it checks the revision.
