## Journeys

No journey was executed end to end. The audit had no running environment: repository rules forbid local databases and devices, the founder's handset was not used, and no deployed environment was driven. Each result below is what the code says would happen, and what a real run needs.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Upload a long video, publish it, play it at each quality on a second account | NOT RUN; expected to work on Android and dev, slowly and fragilely in prod; 4K never selectable | Both clients upload resumably and create the post at once; post-service hides it from everyone but the author until every asset is ready and passed (`post-service/internal/service/processing.go:12-21,90-95`). In prod the forward to `media.events.long` fails and the job runs on the reel loop (reels-3), two lanes for the platform (lv-4), restarted by any rollout (lv-5), looping on OOM (lv-6), possibly over scratch (lv-13) or losing a rung silently (lv-15). Production web HLS may fail CORS (reels-2) | A QA or staging stack with the MSK topic list as in prod, CloudFront and a prod-shaped media worker with ffmpeg; a 20-minute 4K landscape upload; a second account switching each rung on Android and in Chrome and Safari, capturing the rendition requested.
